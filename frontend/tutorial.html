A quick tour of PeterParker Step 1 of 5
01 / Search

Find parking near where you’re going.

Search for a campus building, place, or lot. Choose a destination and PeterParker lists nearby parking by walking distance.

Example: Search “Student Center” to see the closest lots around it.

ON THE MAP
⌕ Student Center
⌖Student CenterChoose as destination↗
Nearby lots will be sorted by walking distance.
02 / Parking colors

Read the map at a glance.

Colored areas and pins show the parking category. The tabs in the side panel let you look at one category at a time.

Example: A blue lot is marked free; a dark gray lot requires a permit. Always check posted signs before parking.

MAP COLOR KEY
FreeOpen to everyone
PermitPermit required
PaidCheck rates
LimitedTime limit or tow risk
03 / Lot details

Check the details before you park.

Select a lot on the map or in the list to see its rules, permit information, rates when available, and distance to your destination. Open Reviews to read or share community tips.

Example: Open a permit lot to check which pass it accepts, then read its reviews.

04 / Directions

Get from here to there.

Choose a destination and tap Directions on a nearby lot. PeterParker shows a driving route to the lot and a walking route to your destination.

Example: Route from your location to a lot, then walk from that lot to Student Center. If location access is off, pick a start point on the map.

05 / Your account

Make PeterParker yours.

Use the gear on the map to open Settings. You can update your username, photo, avatar colors, and password. Your reviews appear beside each place.

Example: Change your avatar color, then head back to the map and rate a lot you know.
